Custom Cabinets in Arp, Texas

Arp grew on farming and the railroad, in a town small enough that everyone knows whose truck is whose. It is thirty-five minutes, so it is an easy run out and back in a day. The houses here are frame and brick houses in town, with land beyond, and the pieces that come from Arp are usually family pieces that have stayed in the same house through three generations.

In a town this size a recommendation carries more weight than any amount of advertising, which is why the work has to speak for itself.

What custom cabinets covers

For Arp customers that means kitchen cabinets, built-in shelving, bathroom vanities, laundry and mudroom storage. Refacing solid cabinet boxes costs a fraction of a tear-out. When boxes are past saving, new ones get built to the room rather than ordered to the nearest standard size.

Why furniture round here needs the work

A piece that has sat in a garage or a barn near Arp for a decade is rarely ruined. Dirt and a dead finish look far worse than they are. Cleaning back to bare wood tells you what you actually have.

How to start

The way it works is simple. Send a photo, or bring the piece to the shop in Kilgore. You get an honest answer on whether it is worth doing and what it will cost, before any work starts. If a piece is not worth the money, we will say so — that has cost us jobs and kept customers.

Questions from Arp customers

Do you come out to Arp?

Yes. Arp is thirty-five minutes from the shop in Kilgore, and pickup and delivery can be arranged for anything too big to move yourself.

How much will it cost?

It depends entirely on the piece, which is why nothing is quoted sight unseen. Send a photo and you will get a real number before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Depends on the job and what is already in the shop. You will be told a realistic time up front, not an optimistic one.

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

Sometimes replacing is the right answer and you will be told so. Anything built before about 1980 is usually worth keeping — it was made better than what would replace it.

Pickup and delivery

Arp is thirty-five minutes away, so pickup and delivery can be arranged for anything you cannot move yourself. A dining table or a chest of drawers is not a one-person job and it is not worth damaging a piece getting it into a car.

Do not wait on it

A loose joint gets worse every time the piece is used, and a lifted edge of veneer catches on something and tears further. The repairs that get expensive are almost always the ones that were small a year ago.

Pieces made to order

A fair bit of the work here is a piece somebody wants built for a particular room or a particular person — a table sized to a space, a sign for a family place. Bring a measurement and an idea and it can be drawn up from there.
