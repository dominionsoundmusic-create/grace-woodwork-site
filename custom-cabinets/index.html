Cabinets made for the room you have

A kitchen in a 1950s East Texas house rarely has a square corner or a level floor. Custom cabinets are measured in the house and built to that space, so they fit the walls instead of being shimmed and filled to look like they do.

We build kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, laundry room and mudroom storage, garage cabinets, home office desks and built-in bookcases. Boxes, face frames, doors and drawers are made in our Kilgore shop, finished, and then installed. You can see cabinet jobs in progress in the gallery: boxes primed in a garage, doors waiting for finish, a home office fitted out wall to wall.

Common questions

What are custom cabinets?

Cabinets built to the measurements of your room rather than picked from a set of standard sizes. Stock and semi-custom lines come in fixed widths with filler strips to close the gaps; custom cabinets are made to fit the wall, the ceiling and the quirks of the house.

Are custom cabinets worth it?

They are worth it where standard sizes waste space or do not fit: older houses with walls that are not square, odd corners, high ceilings, or storage that has to work around a window or a chimney. For a straightforward new kitchen, a stock line can be the better value, and we will say so.

How long do custom cabinets take to make?

It depends on the size of the job and what is already in the shop. You get a realistic time when we quote, after we have measured.

Can you repair or update my existing kitchen cabinets instead?

Often. Loose doors, sagging shelves and worn finishes can be repaired, and solid boxes can be painted or refaced with new doors and drawer fronts. Water-damaged particleboard is the main thing that has to be replaced.

How much do custom cabinets cost?

Each job is priced after we measure, because the size, the wood, the doors and the finish all change the number. For painting existing cabinets, see what cabinet painting costs.

Send a photo and we will tell you what it needs.

A phone picture is plenty. You will hear what the piece needs, what it will cost and roughly how long it will take before anything starts.
