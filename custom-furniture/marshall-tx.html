What custom furniture covers

What that involves: dining tables, benches, shelving, headboards and pieces built to fit a room nothing off a shelf will fit. Built to your measurements, in the wood you choose, by the man who will deliver it. No flat pack, no six-week freight, no particle board.

Why furniture round here needs the work

A piece that has sat in a garage or a barn near Marshall for a decade is rarely ruined. Dirt and a dead finish look far worse than they are. Cleaning back to bare wood tells you what you actually have.

Custom Furniture in Marshall, Texas

Marshall grew on the railroad, the pottery trade and a long history as one of the older towns in East Texas. It is forty-five minutes east, so it is an easy run out and back in a day. The houses here are a substantial stock of Victorian and antebellum-era houses, some restored and some waiting, and the pieces that come from Marshall are usually period pieces that belong with the houses — and cabinetry that has to be built to match rooms with high ceilings and deep trim.

Marshall has more genuinely old houses than anywhere nearby. Built-ins there have to be made to the room, because nothing off a shelf fits a house of that age.

Questions from Marshall customers

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

Sometimes replacing is the right answer and you will be told so. Anything built before about 1980 is usually worth keeping — it was made better than what would replace it.

Pickup and delivery

Marshall is forty-five minutes east away, so pickup and delivery can be arranged for anything you cannot move yourself. A dining table or a chest of drawers is not a one-person job and it is not worth damaging a piece getting it into a car.

Do not wait on it

A loose joint gets worse every time the piece is used, and a lifted edge of veneer catches on something and tears further. The repairs that get expensive are almost always the ones that were small a year ago.

Refinishing is not always the answer

Stripping a piece back is sometimes exactly wrong. An original finish, even a tired one, is part of what an older piece is worth, and once it is off it does not come back. Cleaning and reviving what is there is often the better job and the cheaper one.
