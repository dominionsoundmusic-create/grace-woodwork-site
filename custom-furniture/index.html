Furniture built for one room and one family

Custom furniture means starting from your space instead of a catalog. A table that seats everyone at Thanksgiving in a dining room that is a little narrow. A bench that fits the exact wall by the back door. Shelving that goes up to an old house's high ceiling.

Everything is built in our Kilgore shop from solid wood, then stained or painted to suit the room it is going into. We have built farmhouse and pedestal tables, entry benches, bed frames, desks with shelving, bookcases, coat racks and step stools for East Texas families, and you can see many of them in the gallery.

Wood, finish and the East Texas climate

Solid wood moves with the seasons. It swells a little in humid months and shrinks when the air conditioning dries the house out, and a well-built piece is designed to let that happen. Table tops are fastened so they can move. Panels sit loose in their frames. That is the difference between a piece that cracks in its second summer and one that is still flat in twenty years.

Pine is the everyday choice around here: affordable, easy to work, and good with a stain. Harder woods like oak cost more and dent less. We will talk through what makes sense for how the piece will be used, and match a stain to floors or furniture already in the room.

Common questions

What custom furniture do you build?

Dining and farmhouse tables, coffee and console tables, entry benches, bed frames, desks, bookcases, shelving, storage boxes, step stools and coat racks are all pieces that have come out of the shop. If you can describe it or sketch it, send it over and we will tell you whether we can build it.

Can you build from a picture or my own drawing?

Yes. A photo of a piece you like, or a drawing with rough measurements, is a good place to start. We work out the sizes, the wood and the joinery with you before anything is cut, and you see a price first.

How much does custom furniture cost?

It depends on the size, the wood, the joinery and the finish, so every piece is quoted on its own. Send the size you need and a picture of the look you want, and you will get a real number.

Is custom furniture worth it compared with buying from a store?

When you need a size a store does not sell, want solid wood instead of veneered board, or want a piece that will be repaired rather than thrown away in ten years, it usually is. When a store piece fits your room and budget, we will tell you that too.

Do you deliver custom furniture?

Yes. We deliver around Kilgore, Longview, Tyler and the rest of the towns we work in, and a long table can be built to come apart so it fits through an older doorway.

Send a photo and we will tell you what it needs.

A phone picture is plenty. You will hear what the piece needs, what it will cost and roughly how long it will take before anything starts.
