What Size Dining Table Seats 8?

For eight people, most sizing guides land on a rectangular table about 84 to 96 inches long, or a round table about 72 inches across. Here is how those numbers are worked out, and how to check your room before you buy or build.

The short answer

Eight people at a rectangular table need a top about 84 to 96 inches long and 36 to 40 inches wide. At a round table, 72 inches across seats eight comfortably. Sources differ by a few inches, and the right size for you depends on your chairs and your room.

The numbers come from one simple rule: allow about 24 inches of table edge for each person. Three people down each long side of a 96-inch table get 32 inches each, with one more person at each end. On an 84-inch table, they get closer to 28 inches.

Width matters too. Most dining tables are 36 to 40 inches wide, and some guides go to 42. Standard dining height is 28 to 30 inches, with 30 the most common.

Check the room before the table

A table that seats eight is only useful if people can get into the chairs. The National Kitchen and Bath Association's planning guidelines give clear numbers for the space around a table:

  • 36 inches from the table edge to a wall or cabinet where nobody needs to walk behind seated diners
  • 44 inches where people need to walk past someone who is seated
  • 60 inches where a wheelchair needs to pass

So a 96 by 40 inch table with 36 inches all around needs a room at least 168 by 112 inches, about 14 feet by 9 feet 4 inches, before you account for a sideboard or a doorway. Mark the table's outline on the floor with painter's tape and pull the chairs out to see how it feels.

Shape, base and leaves

Rectangle or round? A rectangle seats the most people in a long room and suits a family that sits in the same places every night. A round or oval table puts everyone in conversation and has no corners to bump in a tight space, but a big round table makes it hard to reach the middle.

Legs or pedestal? Legs at the corners can get in the way of the end seats. A trestle or a pedestal base frees up knee room, which helps when you are squeezing in an extra chair.

Leaves. A table that seats six every day and eight or ten at holidays saves floor space the rest of the year. Plan the leaves into the base from the start so the table stays steady when it is extended.

Common questions

What size table seats 8 people?

A rectangular table about 84 to 96 inches long and 36 to 40 inches wide, or a round table about 72 inches in diameter. A 60-inch round can fit eight, but it is a tight squeeze.

What are the dining table dimensions for 6?

Common guidance is a rectangle about 72 to 78 inches long, a round table about 60 inches across, or an oval about 60 to 66 inches long. Six people at 24 inches each fit comfortably on a 72-inch rectangle with two at the ends.

How much space does each person need at a dining table?

Plan on about 24 inches of table edge per person. Many guides call 24 to 30 inches comfortable, and armchairs need the upper end of that range.

How much room do I need around a dining table?

The National Kitchen and Bath Association recommends 36 inches from the table edge to a wall where nobody walks behind the chairs, 44 inches where people need to walk past seated diners, and 60 inches for a wheelchair.

What size table seats 10?

At 24 inches per person, ten people fit on about 96 inches of rectangular table with one person at each end, or 120 inches with five down each side and nobody at the ends. That is our arithmetic from the per-person figure rather than a published standard; a table with a leaf is one way to get there.
