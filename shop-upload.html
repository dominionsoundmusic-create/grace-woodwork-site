Before you take the picture

  • Get it into good light. Outside in the shade is best, or right by an open shop door. Overhead shop lights alone make the wood look orange.
  • Don't shoot into a window or the sun — the piece goes dark and the background goes white.
  • Wipe it down first. Sawdust and fingerprints show up far more in a photo than in person.
  • Get the whole piece in frame with a little room around it, and hold the phone level so the legs don't lean.
  • Take two: one straight on, one from the corner so it looks solid rather than flat.
  • Clear what's behind it if you can. A plain wall or the shop floor beats a cluttered bench.
