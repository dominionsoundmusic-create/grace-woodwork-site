What we do

Six things we're known for.

RESTORATION

Furniture restoration

Dining tables and chairs, bedroom sets, dressers and desks. Stripped, repaired, stained and sealed — keeping the piece you already love.

BUILD

Custom tables

Farmhouse and pedestal tables built to your room and your seating. Solid wood, your choice of finish, made to outlast the house.

BUILD

Benches, shelves & cabinets

Bookshelves, entry benches, storage cabinets and built-ins, sized to the space instead of the store.

Kitchen cabinetry

New cabinet builds and installs, plus refinishing and door replacement when the boxes are still good.

Framing, decking, railing and siding repair. The same joinery standards we use indoors, built for East Texas weather.

Signs & custom frames

Seasonal, family, scripture and welcome signs, laundry-room sets, coat racks, key holders, metal Texas flags and made-to-measure frames — sold under the Stella Grace name.

The shop

Everything leaves here by hand.

Grace Woodwork is a working shop in Kilgore. Pieces come in on a trailer, get taken apart on the bench, and go back out finished — no production line, no shortcuts, and no guessing at what you wanted. The same hands that build it are the ones that load it onto the truck.

  • Free estimate before any work starts
  • Photos sent as the piece comes along
  • Pickup and delivery across East Texas
  • Repairs to joints, veneer, legs and hardware
  • Stain matched to what's already in your room
