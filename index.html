Common questions

Do you repair old furniture, or only build new pieces?

Both. Repair and refinishing covers loose chairs, water-ringed tables, lifted veneer and tired finishes on family pieces. Building to order covers dining tables, benches, beds, cabinets, built-in shelves and signs. Tell us which you need, or send a photo if you are not sure.

How do I find out what a job will cost?

Send a photo with the quote form below or by Facebook Messenger, or call 903-445-7477. A phone picture is usually enough to say whether it is a simple fix or a bigger job. You get a real number before any work starts, and nothing is quoted sight unseen.

Which towns do you work in?

The shop is in Kilgore. We take work from Longview, White Oak, Gladewater, Overton, Troup, Henderson, Tyler, Marshall, Lindale, Big Sandy, Gilmer, Arp, New London and Hallsville, and we have driven as far as Lufkin when the job was worth the trip.

Can you paint or reface my kitchen cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes. If the cabinet boxes are solid, painting them or refacing them with new doors and drawer fronts keeps the layout you have and avoids a full tear-out. See cabinet painting and cabinet refacing.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. You can send a photo through the website or on Facebook any time, and we answer during shop hours.
