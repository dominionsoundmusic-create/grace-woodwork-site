Real wood, your words

A custom wood sign has grain you can see and weight you can feel. It is cut, sanded, lettered and finished in our Kilgore shop, and made to hang somewhere for years rather than be swapped out next season.

We make family name signs and established signs, welcome signs for the front door, scripture pieces, laundry room and kitchen sets, holiday and seasonal signs, address signs, and signs for shops, churches and market stalls. Smaller pieces like coat racks and key holders come out of the same corner of the shop.

Signs are sold under the Stella Grace name. You can order one of the designs we already make, or start from your own wording and size.

Common questions

Can you make a sign with my own wording?

Yes. Most custom wood signs we make start with the customer's own words: a family last name, an established date, a verse, a welcome, a house number or a business name. Send the wording and the size you have in mind.

What size can a custom wood sign be?

Anything from a small shelf sign to a large outdoor name sign. The size is set by where it is going: measure the wall or the spot by the door, or send a photo of it, and we will suggest a size and layout that can be read from where people will stand.

Can a wood sign go outside?

Yes, if it is built for it. An outdoor sign needs wood and a finish chosen for sun and rain, and it should be hung where water can drain off it. Tell us whether it is going under a porch roof or out in the open.

What is Stella Grace?

Stella Grace is the name Grace Woodwork's signs are sold under: seasonal, family, scripture and welcome signs, laundry room sets and similar pieces, as well as custom orders.

Do you make custom frames too?

Yes. Frames are cut to fit the piece, including old photographs, certificates, flags and art in sizes no standard frame takes.

Send a photo and we will tell you what it needs.

A phone picture is plenty. You will hear what the piece needs, what it will cost and roughly how long it will take before anything starts.
