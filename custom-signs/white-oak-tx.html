What custom signs covers

Custom signs work covers carved and painted wood signs for homes, businesses, farms and gifts. Real wood, cut and finished by hand, made to live outdoors in East Texas weather rather than fade in one summer.

Why furniture round here needs the work

Wood moves with the seasons here more than people expect. Drawers that stick in summer and rattle in winter are doing exactly what wood does; the fix is usually adjustment rather than replacement.

Custom Signs in White Oak, Texas

White Oak grew on oilfield work and a school district that keeps families in town for decades. It is ten minutes from the shop, so it is an easy run out and back in a day. The houses here are settled brick homes on generous lots, many owned by the same family since they were built, and the pieces that come from White Oak are usually built-ins and kitchen cabinets from the seventies and eighties that are solid underneath and just look tired.

A lot of White Oak kitchens do not need replacing. New doors, new hardware and a proper refinish costs a fraction of a tear-out and keeps cabinet boxes that were built better than most new ones.

How to start

No estimate is given without seeing the piece, and none is padded to be safe. Photos first, then a straight price. If it would cost more to fix than to replace, you will hear that instead of a quote.

Questions from White Oak customers

Is it worth repairing or should I replace it?

Sometimes replacing is the right answer and you will be told so. Anything built before about 1980 is usually worth keeping — it was made better than what would replace it.

Do you come out to White Oak?

Yes. White Oak is ten minutes from the shop from the shop in Kilgore, and pickup and delivery can be arranged for anything too big to move yourself.

How much will it cost?

It depends entirely on the piece, which is why nothing is quoted sight unseen. Send a photo and you will get a real number before any work starts.

How long does it take?

Small repairs are usually days. A full refinish or a built-in is longer, and you will be given a date rather than a vague answer.

Pickup and delivery

White Oak is ten minutes from the shop away, so pickup and delivery can be arranged for anything you cannot move yourself. A dining table or a chest of drawers is not a one-person job and it is not worth damaging a piece getting it into a car.

Refinishing is not always the answer

Stripping a piece back is sometimes exactly wrong. An original finish, even a tired one, is part of what an older piece is worth, and once it is off it does not come back. Cleaning and reviving what is there is often the better job and the cheaper one.
