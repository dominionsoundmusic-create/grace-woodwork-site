Furniture built for one room and one family

Custom furniture means starting from your space instead of a catalog. A table that seats everyone at Thanksgiving in a dining room that is a little narrow. A bench that fits the exact wall by the back door. Shelving that goes up to an old house's high ceiling.

Everything is built in our Kilgore shop from solid wood, then stained or painted to suit the room it is going into. We have built farmhouse and pedestal tables, entry benches, bed frames, desks with shelving, bookcases, coat racks and step stools for East Texas families, and you can see many of them in the gallery.

Wood, finish and the East Texas climate

Solid wood moves with the seasons. It swells a little in humid months and shrinks when the air conditioning dries the house out, and a well-built piece is designed to let that happen. Table tops are fastened so they can move. Panels sit loose in their frames. That is the difference between a piece that cracks in its second summer and one that is still flat in twenty years.

Pine is the everyday choice around here: affordable, easy to work, and good with a stain. Harder woods like oak cost more and dent less. We will talk through what makes sense for how the piece will be used, and match a stain to floors or furniture already in the room.
