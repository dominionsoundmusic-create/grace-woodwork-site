Cabinets made for the room you have

A kitchen in a 1950s East Texas house rarely has a square corner or a level floor. Custom cabinets are measured in the house and built to that space, so they fit the walls instead of being shimmed and filled to look like they do.

We build kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, laundry room and mudroom storage, garage cabinets, home office desks and built-in bookcases. Boxes, face frames, doors and drawers are made in our Kilgore shop, finished, and then installed. You can see cabinet jobs in progress in the gallery: boxes primed in a garage, doors waiting for finish, a home office fitted out wall to wall.
