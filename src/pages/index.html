A woodworking shop that fixes what you have and builds what you need

Grace Woodwork is a working shop in Kilgore with three or four people at the benches. Furniture comes in tired, loose or water-marked and goes home sound. Tables, cabinets, shelves and signs get built to the room they are going into rather than to a catalog size.

Most jobs start the same way: a phone picture of the piece or the space, a conversation about what it needs, and a price before anything is taken apart. Pick the kind of work below to see how we handle it.

Everything leaves here by hand

Grace Woodwork is a working shop in Kilgore. Pieces come in on a trailer, get taken apart on the bench, and go back out finished. There is no production line, and no guessing at what you wanted. The same people who build or repair a piece are the ones who load it onto the truck.

  • A price before any work starts
  • Photos sent as the piece comes along
  • Pickup and delivery across East Texas
  • Repairs to joints, veneer, legs and hardware
  • Stain matched to what is already in your room
