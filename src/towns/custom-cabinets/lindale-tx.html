New-build kitchens have a standard set of shortcomings

In a town growing as fast as Lindale, most kitchens came with the house and were specified by a builder working to a price across a whole subdivision. They usually look fine on the day and fall short in the same predictable ways: a pantry too shallow to hold a week's shopping, no drawer deep enough for pans, a corner unit nobody can reach into, and drawer runners rated well below what a family loads onto them.

None of that needs a new kitchen. It needs the two or three units that fail replaced with ones built properly, which is a far smaller job than people assume and reads as a whole new kitchen once it is done.

What a builder leaves out

The bigger opportunity in a newer house is what was never there. A mudroom bench with hooks, cubbies and boot storage. A proper pantry run. Laundry cabinetry so the room is more than a corridor with a machine in it. A built-in desk for whoever works from home. Storage under the stairs. Garage shelving that will hold weight. Window seats in the bay that is currently empty.

That work costs less than a kitchen and changes a house more, because it is what stops everything living on the floor and on the counter.

Built to a different standard than what came with the house

What we build is not builder-grade. Carcasses in plywood rather than particle board, drawer boxes jointed rather than stapled, runners rated for real loads, doors that will not swell at the bottom edge the first time something spills. It costs more than the original units did, and it is meant to be the last set the house needs.

Working with builders and remodellers

Construction is one of the largest employers in Lindale, and we are happy to work as the joinery on somebody else's job rather than as the main contractor. If you are a builder or remodeller who needs cabinetry made to size for a client, that is straightforward work for us and we will quote to your program rather than ours.
