Signs that have to last without maintenance

Troup sits at the crossing of two state highways and four farm roads in the corner of Smith County, with the city limits running down into Cherokee County. Most of what we make for this area goes up outdoors and a long way from anybody who is going to look after it, gate signs, farm names, place names at the end of a drive.

That makes construction matter more than decoration. Timber chosen for outdoors, joints cut rather than stapled, lettering with enough depth that it still reads once the surface has weathered, and a finish that will take an East Texas summer. Tell us how exposed the spot is and how far it sits from the house.

Church signs and noticeboards

Troup had four churches when it had six hundred people, and small-town East Texas has not changed much in that respect. Church signs, noticeboards and scripture pieces are steady work here, and they are the sort of thing that gets made once and stays up for decades, which is a good argument for timber over something printed.

Made in a furniture shop

These are timber pieces, not printing. Real frames, real boards, joints cut rather than stapled, and a finish put on the way a table gets finished. That is why they survive on a gatepost, and why they sit properly beside good furniture indoors instead of looking like gift shop stock.

Family and house pieces

Family names and established dates. House numbers. Scripture. Nursery and children's room pieces. Wedding and anniversary signs. Memorial pieces for a family place. Kitchen and laundry wording. One sign for one room is a perfectly normal order.

Coat racks and hooks

A close cousin of the sign work and one of the most-asked-for things in the shop. A stained board with cast hooks, cut to the length of wall you actually have, sorts out a back door or a mud room that never had anywhere to hang anything. They take lettering too.

Ordering

Send the wording, the rough size, and a photograph of where it is going. You get a price before anything is cut, and Troup is about forty minutes from the shop.
