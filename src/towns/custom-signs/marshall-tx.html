A two-month season, and everybody is looking up

From late November to New Year's Day, Wonderland of Lights covers the 1901 courthouse and the whole downtown square in lights, with an ice rink, a vintage carousel, carriage rides and a Christmas market. Visitor numbers dwarf the town's twenty-odd thousand residents, and every one of those people is walking the square on foot, at night, looking at buildings.

That is the best and the worst possible test of a shop sign. Lit well, a painted timber board with real depth reads beautifully against old brick. A printed panel under the same lights looks exactly like what it is.

Order it in summer, not in November

The practical advice for anyone trading on the square: a sign wanted for the Wonderland season should be ordered months ahead. October is when every trade in Marshall is booked solid, and a rushed exterior sign is a badly finished one. Timber signs need the finish to cure properly if they are going to survive outdoors, and that is not something to hurry.

Tell us where it is going and how exposed the spot is. An exterior sign gets built for outdoors rather than being an indoor sign hung outside, and that is a different construction, not just a different coat of paint.

Made in a furniture shop

These are timber pieces, not printing. Real frames, real boards, joints cut rather than stapled, finished the way a table gets finished. That is why they last on a frontage through an East Texas summer, and why they sit properly against nineteenth-century brick instead of floating in front of it.

Away from the square

Family names and established dates. House numbers. Scripture. Nursery and children's room pieces. Wedding and anniversary signs. Memorial pieces for a family place. Kitchen and laundry wording. One sign for one room is a perfectly normal order, and so is a set for a whole shopfront.

Coat racks and hooks

A close cousin of the sign work and one of the most-asked-for things in the shop. A stained board with cast hooks, cut to the length of wall you actually have, sorts out a back door or a mudroom that never had anywhere to hang anything. They take lettering too, which makes them a sign and a coat rack at once.

Ordering from Marshall

Send the wording, the rough size and a photograph of where it is going, on the square, the brick color and the light decide what will read. You get a price before anything is cut. Marshall is about forty minutes from the shop on I-20.
