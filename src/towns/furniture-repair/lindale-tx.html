A town where most people have recently arrived

Lindale has grown faster than anywhere else we serve. The population has gone up by roughly a hundred and forty per cent since 2000, and it is still climbing, a town of under three thousand at the turn of the century is over seven thousand now, spread across new subdivisions north of Tyler at the crossroads of Interstate 20 and Highway 69.

Growth like that means moving vans, and moving vans mean damaged furniture. A dining table that travelled from Dallas with a leg strapped badly. A wardrobe that came off a truck harder than it went on. A chest that sat in storage for three months in a Texas summer and came out with the veneer lifting. That is the most common reason somebody in Lindale calls us.

Moving damage is usually cheaper to fix than people fear

The instinct after a bad move is to write the piece off, and it is normally the wrong instinct. A snapped leg can be repaired invisibly far more often than replaced. A joint that racked in transit needs taking apart and re-gluing, not a new frame. Veneer that lifted in storage will usually go back down.

Photograph the damage before you decide anything, particularly if a mover's insurance is involved, and get a repair quote before you accept a settlement, because the repair figure is frequently a fraction of the replacement one and the difference is yours to keep.

And the pieces that came with the family

The other half of the work is what people bring with them and want right before it goes into a new house: a grandmother's chest, a dining set that has moved three times, a rocking chair somebody was given. Those get repaired, and often refinished to suit a lighter, newer room, which is a legitimate thing to do, though we will say first if a piece looks old enough that stripping it might matter to somebody later.

What comes in

Loose and broken joints. Chairs that rock. Drawers that bind, drop or sit crooked. Lifted and blistered veneer. Water rings, heat marks and sun-faded tops. Split boards and cracked legs. Missing moldings cut to match rather than ordered. Full strip-and-refinish work where a piece is genuinely ready for it.

Fifty minutes down the road

The shop is in Kilgore, about fifty minutes east of Lindale. That is the furthest of the towns we cover regularly and it is still a straightforward run, we collect and return rather than expecting you to move a wardrobe. Start with a photo; it is usually enough for a real number before anything begins.
