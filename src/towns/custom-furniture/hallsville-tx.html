The school is the town

Hallsville has grown quickly in the last decade, a town that had one traffic light until 2003 now has six, a larger grocery, clinics and the rest of it. But the thing everybody says about it has not changed: the heart and pulse of the community is in its school functions, particularly the sport.

That shapes what people need built. Long tables that seat a lot of people, because the gatherings here are big ones. Bench seating rather than chairs, for the same reason. Somewhere for equipment and kit to live. Trophy and display shelving that will hold weight rather than bow in the middle after a season.

Tables built for a crowd

A dining table that seats ten is not a scaled-up version of one that seats six. It needs more leg support without putting a leg where a knee goes, a thicker top so it does not deflect in the middle, and a way to get it through your door, which on a long table often means building it to come apart.

Those are all solvable, and they are exactly the things a shop-bought table does not consider. Tell us how many people you actually need to seat at Christmas rather than on a Tuesday, and it gets built for the Christmas number.

Church and community furniture

Hallsville has run its life through its churches since there were three of them serving six hundred people in 1884. Those buildings need furniture nobody sells in a shop, communion tables, lecterns, pew repairs and extensions, and fellowship hall tables that seat properly and store flat. We do that work and quote it plainly, because it usually comes out of a committee budget rather than a household one.

There is a crew here, not one pair of hands

Grace Woodwork is several people, which is the difference between taking a job with a fixed date on it and turning it down. Eight matching hall tables do not take eight times as long as one.

How a build starts

With a measurement and an idea, and a photograph of the room. Then timber, finish, price and timing, and nothing is cut until you have a real number. Hallsville is about thirty-five minutes from the shop, and we deliver and install.
