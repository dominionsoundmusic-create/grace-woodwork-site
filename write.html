Write a post

Anything you publish here goes straight onto the website and shows up in the blog.

What works

  • Write about one piece. What came in, what was wrong with it, what you did, how it turned out.
  • Use the words a customer would. "Water ring on a dining table" beats "surface remediation."
  • Say where. Mentioning Kilgore, Longview or Tyler helps people nearby find it.
  • A few short paragraphs is plenty. Leave a blank line between them.
  • Add a photo if you have one — the finished piece is the best one to use.
